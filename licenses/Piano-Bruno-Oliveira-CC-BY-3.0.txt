This app includes an adapted native mesh: colors adjusted, lid/support separated
for movement, 88-key keyboard, individual strings, cast frame, tuning pins, soundboard ribs and pedal hardware added. The 0.17 native mesh has 3962 triangles (475452 bytes), no textures. Original author has
not endorsed this app. The model depicts a stylized grand piano, not Yamaha P-145BT.

License permits sharing/adaptation with attribution and indication of changes;
no additional restrictions are imposed on this asset. This notice, source and
license links must accompany redistributions of the mesh.

Original GLB and deterministic preparation script are in the source project.
