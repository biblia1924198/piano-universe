Selected mono 48kHz measured responses: Room6, Room9 and Room12, Speak1_Mic1.
Adaptations: align direct peak; suppress first 2ms to remove direct sound; 1ms onset fade; 8ms wet predelay; retain first 80ms including predelay; remove DC; 5.33ms end fade; normalize impulse energy to 0.28; linearly resample to 44.1/96kHz. Exact coefficients included in native/room_ir.h, originals in build-support/room-ir-source.
The early reflections are measured. The stereo late tail and shimmer are original synthesized DSP; this is not a complete convolution recreation of the original rooms. No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
